📖 Texas Hold'em Rules

Objective

Make the best 5-card poker hand using your 2 hole cards and the 5 community cards.

Hand Rankings (Highest to Lowest)

  • Royal Flush - A, K, Q, J, 10 of same suit
  • Straight Flush - Five sequential cards, same suit
  • Four of a Kind - Four cards of same rank
  • Full House - Three of a kind + a pair
  • Flush - Five cards of same suit
  • Straight - Five sequential cards
  • Three of a Kind - Three cards of same rank
  • Two Pair - Two different pairs
  • One Pair - Two cards of same rank
  • High Card - Highest card wins

Betting Rounds

  1. Pre-Flop - After receiving hole cards
  2. Flop - First 3 community cards
  3. Turn - 4th community card
  4. River - 5th community card
